Entrepreneur · Founder of Panhelios

I founded Panhelios in 2010 and brought a software development kit for games onto the market in 2012. Over the following years I launched four social games in cooperation with international partners. By the time I handed over the company in 2017, over seven million players had enjoyed a game under the Panhelios flag.

About the SDK

The Panhelios Software Development Kit enables small teams to produce modern browser games. We used the SDK in the production of all our games.

  • Model-driven development to reduce production overhead.
  • Object-relational mapping for easy access of game data.
  • HelioScript translates to PHP and JavaScript code, so all code can run on the server and the client side in sync.
  • Professional IDE: Eclipse.
  • Supports WebSocket and AJAX.
  • Modern HTML5 graphics engine.

About the game

Cook Hero takes you to a cooking academy where you will learn the basics of matching dishes, and begin to collect your recipes.

  • Unlock and collect real recipes.
  • A unique addition to the match-3 formula: gravity changes with each level!
